How Our Plumbers Determine the Exact Drainage Solution

When you call Drain Cleaning Advisor, our technicians do not guess which tool to run. We follow a rigorous four-phase diagnostic protocol tailored to Collier County residential and commercial plumbing systems:

Phase 1: Flow Symptom Assessment

Is only one fixture slow (pointing to an isolated trap obstruction), or are multiple fixtures gurgling (confirming a mainline sewer obstruction)?

Phase 2: Cleanout Access & Video Scope

We access the lateral via outdoor ground cleanout, pull the camera through, and show you real-time footage of root mats, grease, or collapsed pipe joints.

Phase 3: Written Quote & Authorization

We present a flat written rate before work begins. If a standard cable will clear it, we quote snaking; if heavy roots require scouring, we quote jetting.

Phase 4: Flow Re-Verification & Video Confirmation

After clearing, we run water volume tests and re-camera the line so you see the pristine internal pipe walls before our van leaves your driveway.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know which drain service I need?

If a single sink, tub, or shower is slow, drain snaking and cabling is typically sufficient. If multiple fixtures are backing up or toilets are gurgling, main sewer line cleaning is needed. For recurring grease, sand, or commercial kitchen lines, hydro jetting provides complete 360-degree pipe scouring.

Do you provide video pipe inspection with every service?

Yes, we run our high-definition camera on lines before authorizing mechanical cutting or hydro jetting. This verifies whether the blockage is organic or a damaged pipe joint.

Do you offer emergency drain cleaning on weekends in Naples?

Yes, our Naples dispatch desk operates 7 days a week for active backups and urgent sewage blockages across Collier County.

What should I expect during a diagnostic video camera pipe inspection in Naples?

Our technician will locate your exterior sewer cleanout or access point and feed a flexible push-rod equipped with a high-definition, self-leveling sapphire lens camera head down your line. As the camera travels through your pipes, you view the live color monitor screen alongside our technician. Why do older Naples homes experience frequent cast-iron drain stoppages?

Many classic residences built between 1950 and 1975 in Old Naples, Aqualane Shores, and Coquina Sands feature original cast-iron waste stacks installed beneath concrete slabs. Over 50+ years of exposure to hydrogen sulfide sewer gases and acidic wastewater, cast iron undergoes a process called tuberculation, where internal rust blisters and jagged scale erupt on the pipe interior. These rough rust formations act like velcro, catching toilet paper and food fibers that would otherwise wash smoothly through modern PVC pipe. Our specialized mechanical cabling and hydro descaling techniques gently smooth out these rust ridges without fracturing fragile pipe walls.

Collier County Plumbing Standards & Safety Codes

All drain cleaning procedures performed by Drain Cleaning Advisor comply strictly with Collier County building codes, Florida Building Code (Plumbing), and municipal environmental mandates designed to safeguard our Gulf of Mexico coastal waterways from cross-contamination and sanitary sewer overflows.
