Privacy Policy

Drain Cleaning Advisor is committed to protecting your privacy and diagnostic plumbing records. This policy outlines how customer contact details, video diagnostics, and service requests are securely managed.

Last Updated: October 2026
FDUTPA & FDBR Compliant

1. Introduction & Scope of Privacy Policy

Drain Cleaning Advisor ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") operates as a specialized drain cleaning and sewer clearing contractor serving residential homeowners, luxury estates, condominium associations, and commercial hospitality establishments throughout Naples and Collier County, Florida. This Privacy Policy discloses our privacy practices and governs how personal information, technical diagnostic records, and digital communications are collected, processed, secured, and retained across our website (draincleaningnaplesflorida.com), dispatch call center, and on-site field tablet software.

We are dedicated to safeguarding your privacy in full compliance with the Florida Digital Bill of Rights (FDBR), the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA), and applicable state and federal data privacy standards. By utilizing our website, requesting a diagnostic estimate, or booking plumbing dispatch, you acknowledge and agree to the data collection and usage practices described herein.

2. Personal and Property Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to fulfill plumbing dispatch requests, provide accurate written quotes, perform video camera sewer diagnostics, and execute authorized clearing procedures:

  • Customer Contact Details: Full legal name, residential property address, commercial facility address, billing mailing address, primary telephone contact numbers, and verified email address.
  • Plumbing Lateral Diagnostics: Digital camera video files, pipe depth measurements, underground sonde location GPS coordinates, pipe material specifications (PVC, cast iron, clay), and historical sewer backup documentation.
  • Transactional Records: Written diagnostic estimates, customer signature authorizations, work orders, payment receipts, warranty certificates, and electronic invoices.
  • Digital Device Data: IP address, device type, browser user-agent string, operating system, and technical session identifiers captured automatically during website interaction.

3. Telecommunications & Dispatch Protocols (Calls & SMS)

Our dispatch desk operates under strict compliance with federal and Florida telecommunications statutes:

  • Telephone Call Recording: Inbound and outbound telephone calls connecting with our dispatch desk at +1 (239) 688-0557 may be recorded and stored for customer service quality assurance, technician dispatch coordination, and work verification.
  • SMS Transactional Messaging: When you provide your mobile telephone number to our dispatch team, you explicitly consent to receive transactional text notifications. These messages are limited strictly to technician estimated time of arrival (ETA) notices, en-route vehicle alerts, and electronic work order delivery. You may opt out of SMS communications at any time by replying "STOP".
  • No Robocalling or Automated Solicitation: We never engage in automated outbound telemarketing robocalls or distribute pre-recorded commercial advertisements to customer telephone numbers.

4. Zero-Sale Commitment (We Never Sell Customer Data)

Drain Cleaning Advisor upholds an unconditional commitment to customer confidentiality. We never sell, rent, monetize, trade, or transfer your personal information, email address, home address, or telephone number to third-party data brokers, commercial advertisers, or lead generation affiliates. Your data is used exclusively by our certified personnel to execute the plumbing services you requested.

5. Payment Processing & Financial Data Security

When you settle an invoice using a credit card, debit card, or electronic bank transfer through our mobile field tablets, your financial information is processed directly by a Level 1 PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway using end-to-end cryptographic tokenization. Our technicians, mobile tablets, and web servers never view, store, or retain unencrypted credit card numbers, magnetic stripe data, or CVV security codes.

6. Website Analytics & Cookie Management

Our website utilizes first-party technical session cookies and anonymized web traffic measurement tools (such as Google Analytics) to assess page loading speed, diagnose broken hyperlinks, and improve local search indexing. These cookies capture aggregated, non-personally identifiable metrics such as device screen resolution, general geographic vicinity (Naples, FL), and referral search keywords. You can instruct your web browser to refuse cookies without disabling any core functionality on our website.

7. Data Retention & Digital Security Safeguards

We maintain comprehensive digital, physical, and administrative safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration. Customer work orders and camera inspection video recordings are stored in secure, encrypted cloud repositories with multi-factor authentication access controls. Records are retained for the duration required to honor multi-year plumbing warranties and satisfy Florida Department of Revenue contractor tax compliance regulations.

8. Florida Consumer Privacy Rights (FDBR Disclosures)

Under Florida law and consumer protection statutes, Florida residents hold enforceable rights concerning their personal information:

  • Right to Know & Access: You may request a summary of the personal and property data we maintain in your customer profile.
  • Right to Correction: You may request the rectification of any inaccurate or outdated contact information in our dispatch system.
  • Right to Deletion: You may request that we delete non-essential contact data, subject to ongoing warranty verification and contractor accounting record mandates.

10. Video Camera Inspection Footage Ownership & Retention

As part of our diagnostic procedures, our technicians record high-definition digital video footage of your property's internal drainage and sewer lateral pipes. This footage is the property of the customer and Drain Cleaning Advisor. We maintain copies of camera diagnostic records in secure cloud storage for a minimum of 24 months. These video files serve as formal verification of pipe condition, providing objective evidence for homeowner insurance claims, municipal sewer responsibility determinations, and prospective real estate buyer disclosures. We never publish or share inspection video recordings publicly without your express written consent.

11. Third-Party Service Providers & Subprocessors

We may engage vetted, third-party technology subprocessors to facilitate business operations, such as cloud data hosting (Amazon Web Services), field service dispatch management software (ServiceTitan), and SMS appointment gateway providers (Twilio). All subprocessors are legally bound by strict data processing agreements that require enterprise-grade encryption, prohibit secondary data use, and mandate compliance with Florida consumer data protection laws.

12. Children's Privacy Protections

Our website and plumbing services are directed exclusively to adult property owners, tenants, and commercial property managers aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or maintain personal information from children under the age of 13 in accordance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). If we learn that personal data of a minor has been inadvertently submitted, we take immediate steps to delete the record from our dispatch systems.
